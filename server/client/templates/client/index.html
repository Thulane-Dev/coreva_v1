Frequently asked questions

Construction software questions, answered.

Straight answers about pricing, daily reports, subcontractors and getting started.

What is Coreva?

Coreva is construction site management software for South African contractors. It brings projects, daily inspections, issues, tasks, photos, subcontractors and daily site reports into one place.

How much does Coreva cost?

Coreva costs R699 per month and supports up to 10 active projects. The plan is for the whole company with no per-user fees. Start with a 14-day free trial with no credit card required.

Can I manage multiple construction projects?

Yes. Coreva gives your company one place to manage multiple active construction projects. The current plan supports up to 10 active projects.

Can subcontractors use Coreva?

Yes. Coreva includes a subcontractor workspace. Subcontractors can access the projects and work assigned to them without paying for their own Coreva subscription. They do not need access to your internal company workspace.

Can Coreva create daily construction reports?

Yes. Coreva's daily reports can capture people onsite, work completed, weather, deliveries, delays, incidents, issues, tasks, notes and photos, with PDF reporting available.

Is my data safe?

Coreva is designed with practical data protection and account security in mind. Your project records remain in your company workspace and access is controlled by user permissions.

Is Coreva designed for South African construction companies?

Coreva is built in South Africa, for South African construction companies, contractors and subcontractors that need a practical way to manage everyday project and site operations.
