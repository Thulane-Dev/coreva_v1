Frequently asked questions

Questions about Coreva?

A few things construction teams usually want to know before getting started.

What is Coreva?

Coreva is construction site management software for South African contractors and construction companies. It brings projects, inspections, issues, tasks, daily reports, photos and subcontractors into one place.

How much does Coreva cost?

Coreva costs R699 per month (excluding VAT) for up to 10 active projects. Start with a 14-day free trial — no credit card required. The current plan includes all the core construction operations features rather than separating them into multiple feature tiers.

Can I manage multiple construction projects?

Yes. Coreva gives your company one place to manage multiple active construction projects. The current plan supports up to 10 active projects.

Can subcontractors use Coreva?

Yes. Coreva includes a contractor workspace for subcontractors. They can access the projects and work assigned to them — across all their contractors — without needing access to your internal company workspace. Subcontractor access is free, forever.

Can Coreva create daily construction reports?

Yes. Coreva's daily reports can capture people onsite, work completed, weather, deliveries, delays, incidents, issues, tasks, notes and photos, with PDF reporting available.

Is my data safe?

Yes. Coreva is POPIA compliant and your project data is securely stored. You own your data and can export it at any time.

Is Coreva designed for South African construction companies?

Coreva is built in South Africa, for South African construction companies, contractors and subcontractors that need a practical way to manage everyday project and site operations.
